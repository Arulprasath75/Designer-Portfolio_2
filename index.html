About

Creative thinking rooted in real product experience.

I hold a BSc (Hons) in Tea Technology and Value Addition from Uva Wellassa University of Sri Lanka. That academic background shaped my design language: I understand materials, production realities, and how packaging contributes to product identity and consumer trust.

My work spans packaging dielines, branded presentation, retail-ready design, and marketing visuals for artisanal and premium products. I care about structure, hierarchy, and storytelling — so each design feels meaningful from first glance to final unboxing.
